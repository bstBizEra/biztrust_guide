Reference · not a stage

The Work Package loop against the nine stages

The roadmap gives every Work Package in every phase the same 24-step loop, from Understand to Next Controlled Iteration, and says it extends the guide's nine-stage lifecycle. This page says which stage owns each step and where that stage carries it, so that the loop and the stages are one process described twice, not two processes.

Rendered from BIZTRUST-PLAN-001.md section 13.1, which records the mapping as the plan's proposal from the roadmap's section 9. The table is held to that section by a test, and every stage citation on it is held to a section the stage page displays under that number. The stage count stays nine: every step has an owning stage.

THE RULE THIS PAGE EXISTS TO KEEP

A step of the loop is not a stage, and a stage is not a step. The nine stages are how a Work Package moves from ENG-G0 to ENG-G8; the loop is the roadmap's list of what happens on the way. Where a step has no section of its own, the page says which rule or record carries it, and where nothing does, it says so and proposes an owner. It adds no tenth stage and no gate.

01

The decision

Why a reference page, not nine stage sections

The loop ticket on the Guide v2 map asked whether the steps the stages do not carry explicitly become stage sections or a reference page. This page is the answer, and the reasons are this track's, not the plan's.

The mapping is one table, and belongs in one place

Twenty-four steps across nine stages read as a lookup. Split into nine sections, one per stage, each would restate the same rule about what a step is, and the guide's duplication test holds the stage pages a hair under its threshold on purpose; nine near-identical sections would trip it, and rightly.

Three of the four unnamed steps are already carried

Independent security review under another name, Assure's security reviewer; evidence collection by the charter's evidence rule and Assure's manifest; feeding knowledge back by the charter's checkpoint and handoff records. A section for each would restate the rule where it already lives. The page links the rule instead.

The one gap gets a proposal, not a section written here

Threat and risk analysis is named on no stage page, though the hub assigns the threat model to Architect and the Definition of Done requires it reviewed. That is the Architect manual's gap to close, on its own ticket, so that the section is written by the manual's rules and not squeezed into a lookup.

Seven names for three stages

Steps 10 to 16 are seven names for what the guide splits across Build, Assure and Release. The roadmap's order inside them is not a sequence the stages impose: Assure's six layers run together, and Release verifies after exposure.

Step 18 belongs to no single stage

Every stage ends in an ENG-Gn exit gate, and that is the gate evaluation the loop names. The phase gates BT-Gn are a different namespace, judged on the phase pages and recorded by a human.

03

The unnamed steps

Four steps no stage page names, and what carries each

The ticket named four steps the stages do not carry explicitly, and the table marks exactly those four. On reading the pages, one is carried under another name, two are carried by a rule and by records rather than sections, and one is a gap.

Independent security review · carried, under another name

Assure's roles table names a security reviewer, and Assure's exit gate is what the review feeds. The loop's word "independent" is the charter's: independent review is the last of the eight Definition of Done items, and Build's exit gate is not satisfiable without its reviewer roles. The name differs and the remit is narrower than the loop's step: Assure's reviewer owns layer 4 for changes touching authentication, tenancy or data access, and whether a package outside that remit needs a security review is the risk tier's to say (Define §06).

Evidence collection · carried by a rule, not a step

The evidence manifest is Assure's output, and every stage's evidence is bound to a revision under AGENTS.md section 9. Making collection a step would suggest evidence is gathered once, at one point, which is the failure the charter's rule exists to prevent.

Feed knowledge and memory · carried by records

Learn refuses memory as an input and reads escape-ledger entries and decision records instead, grading decisions against outcomes; what it learns is carried forward by the charter's checkpoint and handoff records and by each map's Decisions-so-far. A Learn section on memory would describe those records; the page links them.

Threat and risk analysis · a gap

No stage page names a threat model. The hub's lifecycle summary lists it among Architect's outputs and the Definition of Done requires it reviewed; Define carries the data classification and the risk tier that feed it. This track proposes a section on the Architect manual, written by that manual's rules, on its own ticket on the Guide v2 map.

04

Limits

What this page does not do

  • It adds no tenth stage and no gate; the nine stages and the two gate namespaces stand
  • It changes no stage page; where a stage carries a step under another name, the page links the section and leaves the name
  • It does not write the Architect section it proposes; that is the Architect manual's, on its own ticket
  • It does not impose the roadmap's order inside Build, Assure and Release; the stages' own order governs
  • It does not carry the roadmap's team model (section 10) or its cross-cutting tracks; those are PLAN-001 section 12's and the team ticket's
