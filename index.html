02

Foundation

Operating principles

Authority ≠ capability

An agent may possess the technical capability to modify or deploy, but may act only within explicitly granted scope, budget and risk boundaries.

Observation ≠ decision

Agents gather facts; policy evaluates facts; authorized stakeholders make decisions. These records remain separate and independently auditable.

Evidence ≠ assertion

“Tests passed” is not evidence. The authoritative run, immutable source revision, environment, output and verifier identity form the evidence bundle.

Completion ≠ acceptance

Implementation completion starts assurance. A work package closes only after validation, review, authority and evidence gates pass.

Human stakeholders own purpose, risk appetite and approval. Agents own bounded execution, transparent reasoning, verification and escalation.

Team topology

The team is a controlled system of specialized roles. For low-risk work, roles may share an agent runtime; for high-risk decisions, independence is mandatory.

O

Translates authorized outcomes into work packages, routes specialists, controls state transitions and stops on ambiguity.

  • Owns execution plan and dependency graph
  • Never self-grants authority
  • Publishes session checkpoint and handoff

Separation-of-duty rule

The same agent instance must not be the sole author, verifier and approver of a high-impact artifact. At least one independent lens must reproduce the critical evidence.

Release control

Go-live checklists

Architecture readiness 7 controls
  • Bounded contexts and ownership approved
  • ADRs accepted and linked
  • APIs and events versioned
  • State machines deterministic
  • Failure modes and compensations defined
  • Capacity assumptions recorded
  • Architecture council findings resolved
Security readiness 8 controls
  • Tenant isolation mechanically proven
  • Threat model current
  • No critical or high findings
  • Secrets externalized and rotated
  • SBOM and provenance available
  • Backup restoration tested
  • Incident roles assigned
  • Production access reviewed
Operational readiness 8 controls
  • SLOs and error budgets approved
  • Dashboards and alerts verified
  • Runbooks linked from alerts
  • Rollback rehearsed
  • RTO/RPO exercise passed
  • Support and escalation trained
  • Cost guardrails active
  • Release ownership confirmed
Business readiness 6 controls
  • UAT acceptance signed
  • Legal and compliance conditions closed
  • Tenant configuration validated
  • Support communications ready
  • Business KPIs instrumented
  • Go/no-go authority recorded
