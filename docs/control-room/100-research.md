## Critical S01 research

1. Which legal entity acts for each initial tenant and product arrangement?
2. Can it make cover effective, issue certificates under a master policy, or only request insurer confirmation?
3. Who owns product, wording, rating, indication and quote/offer authority?
4. Who bears risk while premium, refund or claims money is held or in transit?
5. Which client-money, bank, commission, remittance and reconciliation rules apply?
6. Which effective-time, timezone, correction and supersession rules apply?
7. Are shares, layers, facilities, certificates, bordereaux or account-current required?
8. Which residency, retention, legal-hold and disclosure obligations apply by data class?

## Evidence standard

Use current primary law and regulation, operative agreements, approved business
records and attributable qualified review. A third-party summary can identify a
question; it cannot settle a jurisdiction-specific legal or accounting answer.
