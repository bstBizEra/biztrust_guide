Delivery lifecycle · Discover

Discover

Discover establishes that a problem is real, that somebody pays for it today, and that its cost can be measured — before anyone describes a solution.

THE RULE THIS STAGE EXISTS TO ENFORCE

No improvement may be claimed without a "before". The baseline is taken here, while it still exists, or never.

Nothing on this page reports a finding about BizTrust

BizTrust is planned. No discovery has been run against the corporate brokerage or the government-linked travel scheme, and holds no measurement of either. Every BizTrust example below is a line of enquiry, not a finding.

01

Preconditions

Entry criteria

A sponsor who will act on the answer

Name the role that will read the outcome and act on it, including by cancelling the work. Discovery reported to nobody changes nothing.

Access to the practitioners

The people who do the work daily must be reachable, not merely represented. With only supervisors available, the workarounds carrying the cost stay invisible.

The solution is not already procured

If the platform is chosen, contracted and staffed, run this as a risk review and label it one. Discovery run to justify a commitment finds the justification.

03

The first discipline

A problem stated as a solution is not a problem statement

The test is falsifiability: if somebody could measure the claim and report the cost as negligible, it is a problem statement. If nothing could contradict it, it is a proposal in a problem's grammar.

A problem statement

  • Names who suffers and what it costs, in a unit somebody already tracks
  • Would still stand if the technology you have in mind had never existed
  • Can be shown wrong — the cost may prove smaller than assumed
  • Prices the do-nothing year, the option actually being competed with

A solution wearing a problem's clothes

  • "We need a multi-tenant platform" — a build, not a harm
  • "The process is inefficient" — no unit, no sufferer, nothing to contradict
  • "Users asked for a dashboard" — a feature standing in for the need
  • "Everything is manual" — a method, not the damage it does
04

The one-way door

Establishing the baseline

Every other artifact here can be revised later. This one has a deadline: the first change anybody makes.

Measure before; never reconstruct after

A baseline assembled from memory once work is under way arrives at whichever number justifies the work. Nobody need be dishonest; recall reshapes around the decision taken.

Carry the method with the number

Sample, window, who read it, on what date, from which source. Without its method a number cannot be re-taken, so it compares to nothing.

Pick a representative window, and record the variance

A fortnight containing a public holiday, or a travel scheme's peak, measures the fortnight. Record the spread too; an average hides the cases that hurt.

Give every indicator a value today

A KPI with no measured starting value is a target, not an indicator. Reading indicators against objectives once a service runs belongs to Operate; pinning where they start belongs here.

06

Grading what limits you

Constraints discovered versus constraints assumed

A constraint bounds the solution. Its grade decides how much weight a later stage may hang on it.

Verified

Traced to something citable — a published rule, a licence condition, a contract clause, a written statement from the party with authority. Record the reference and its date.

Asserted

A person stated it. Record who, and when. It may well be correct, and it is still not a citation.

Assumed

Nobody said it; the team carries it anyway. The dangerous class, because nothing marks it. State each limit aloud, then ask who told you.

Architect lists constraints known and written as an entry criterion, and records none today; this register satisfies it. Freeze against an asserted constraint and, if the assertion fails, the decision re-opens rather than adjusts. For BizTrust these are lines of enquiry; the register begins empty.

  • Jurisdictional and regulatory: which authority supervises insurance intermediation in Laos, and what a broker must record, retain and report
  • Data residency: whether policyholder data may leave the country, and on what terms
  • Currency: LAK handling, foreign-currency premiums, rounding, and the authoritative rate source
  • Language: Lao and English on customer documents, and which version governs
  • Government-linked scheme: procurement rules, reporting obligations, and whether that data may share a tenant boundary with commercial business
THE GRADE IS PART OF THE ENTRY

An ungraded register grades itself: the next reader takes every line as verified. Write the grade, the source and the date, or leave the entry out.

07

What the findings rest on

Evidence sources and their reliability

Record which source each fact came from, so a later reader knows how far to trust it.

SourceGood forWhere it misleads
Direct observationWhat is done, including steps nobody mentionsCostly, small sample, and behaviour shifts while watched
Practitioner interviewThe reasoning behind a workaroundRecall, and the wish to be useful to a visitor
Supervisor interviewIntent, priorities, and who holds which authorityGives the process as designed, not as run
System exports and logsVolume, timing and sequence, at full sampleWork never entered leaves no trace
Complaint and rework recordsWhere harm surfaces, in the customer's wordsUnder-reported; a thin file is not a healthy process
Triangulate on independence, not on agreement

Two sources that agree because they share an origin are one source. A supervisor quoting a report generated from the log you already read has corroborated nothing. Ask what would make both wrong together; a single upstream error means they are not independent.

09

Transition

Exit gate

Discover exits to Define when every line holds. Each names an artifact from §02 and its owner from §08. Validation is a signature on the problem statement, not the absence of pushback.

10

Halt

Stop conditions

  • The problem cannot be stated without naming a technology, and the naming is defended rather than fixed
  • No baseline can be measured, and the work is proposed anyway because the gain will be self-evident
  • An asserted or assumed constraint is already being treated downstream as verified
  • The practitioners have not been reached, and a supervisor's account stands in for theirs
  • An indicator's starting value is unknown and cannot be obtained before the change begins
  • The solution is already procured, staffed or announced, and discovery is run to justify it
  • Both tenants are being discovered as one problem, and the statement fits neither
